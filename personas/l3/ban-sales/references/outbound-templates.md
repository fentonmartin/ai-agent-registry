# Outbound Templates — Responsible Cold Outreach

> Every template is a starting point, not a script. Personalize before sending.
> If you can't personalize a Tier 1 email, don't send it.

---

## 1. Principles

**Relevant** — Reference something specific to the recipient's company, role, or situation.  
**Human** — Write like a person, not a marketing department.  
**Honest** — Don't oversell. Don't claim outcomes you can't prove. Use evidence labels from icp-framework.md.  
**Brief** — Cold emails under 100 words outperform longer ones. Get to the point.  
**One ask** — One CTA per message. Never stack asks.

---

## 2. Email Sequence

### Touch 1 — Cold Intro

**Subject options (A/B test; pick one per batch):**
- `[Company name] + [specific trigger/observation]`
- `Quick question for [first name]`
- `[Shared context or mutual connection]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [First name],

[Personalization: 1 sentence about something specific to their company or role —
a recent hire, funding round, job posting, or content they published.]

Most [RevOps leads / growth teams] at [similar stage / industry] companies
tell us that [specific pain point related to ICP criterion].

We help [company type] solve that by [one-line mechanism] — [reference proof:
e.g., "cut attribution setup time from 3 weeks to 48 hours for teams like [peer company]"].

Worth a 20-minute call to see if there's a fit?

[Your name]
[Title] | [Company]
```

**Personalization slots:**
- `[Personalization]` — trigger event, company news, specific content they published
- `[specific pain point]` — must be ICP-verified, not generic
- `[peer company]` — use a reference customer in same industry/stage if possible

**Do NOT:**
- Use "I hope this email finds you well"
- Start with "My name is…"
- List more than one problem or outcome
- Use jargon the buyer wouldn't use themselves

---

### Touch 2 — Follow-up 1 (Day 4–5)

**Subject:** `Re: [original subject]` (keep thread)

**Body:**

```
Hi [First name],

Following up in case this got buried.

[Add one new piece of value: a stat, a brief case study sentence, or a relevant insight
specific to their industry or growth stage.]

Still happy to share how [specific outcome] works — or if this isn't the right time,
just let me know and I'll reach out in [N months].

[Your name]
```

**Do NOT:**
- Say "Just checking in"
- Repeat the entire first email
- Sound passive-aggressive about not replying

---

### Touch 3 — Follow-up 2 (Day 10–12)

**Subject:** New subject line — something specific

```
Hi [First name],

Tried you a couple times — one last thought before I stop.

[One-sentence specific insight relevant to their role: e.g., "Teams scaling RevOps
from 1 to 3 people often hit the same wall around attribution consistency — here's
a 2-minute read on how [peer company] handled it: [link]."]

If now isn't the moment, no worries at all — I'll keep an eye on [Company name]
and circle back when timing makes more sense.

[Your name]
```

---

### Touch 4 — Breakup (Day 18–21)

**Subject:** `Closing the loop`

**Body:**

```
Hi [First name],

I've reached out a few times without hearing back — I'll assume the timing isn't right
and take you off my list.

If [specific pain you mentioned] becomes a priority in the future, I'm easy to find.
Wishing [Company name] a strong [quarter / year].

[Your name]
```

**Do NOT:**
- Be passive-aggressive ("I guess you're not interested…")
- Ask "did I do something wrong?"
- Leave the door closed — keep tone warm and open

## 3. LinkedIn Templates

### Connection Request (300 char limit)

**Option A — Shared context:**
```
Hi [First name] — noticed [specific observation: e.g., "you recently joined [Company]
as Director of RevOps"]. Would love to connect — we work with teams at similar stages
on [one-line problem]. Happy to share what we're seeing.
```

**Option B — Content engagement:**
```
Hi [First name] — read your post on [topic]. Strong perspective on [specific point].
Would love to connect — we think about similar problems at [Your company].
```

**Do NOT:**
- Send a blank connection request to Tier 1 accounts
- Pitch in the connection request itself
- Use "I came across your profile and was impressed"

---

### InMail (use only if email bounced or no email available)

**Subject:** `[Specific trigger or observation]`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

[1 sentence personalization — same rules as email Touch 1.]

We help [company type like theirs] [specific outcome] — [one proof point].

Open to a 20-minute call? Happy to share what's worked for [peer company in their space].

[Your name]
[Title] | [Company]
```

**InMail rules:**
- Use only after 2 unanswered emails, or when email is unavailable
- Keep under 120 words
- Same personalization standard as Tier 1 email

## 4. Trigger-Based Variants

### New Funding Announcement

**Subject:** `Congrats on the [Series X] — quick question`

```
Hi [First name],

Congrats on the [Series B] — great signal for what [Company name] is building.

Growth rounds often mean scaling [revenue / growth / marketing] teams fast,
which is usually when attribution and pipeline visibility become the urgent problem.

We've helped [2–3 peer companies post-raise] get that sorted in weeks, not quarters.

Worth a quick call to compare notes?

[Your name]
```

---

### Hiring Spike (RevOps or Growth roles)

**Subject:** `[Company name]'s RevOps build-out`

```
Hi [First name],

Saw [Company name] is hiring [role] — looks like you're scaling the [RevOps / Growth] function.

Teams at that stage often hit a wall with [specific operational pain — e.g., manual
reporting, attribution gaps, CRM data quality]. We built something specifically for that moment.

Happy to share what's worked for others at your stage — 20 minutes?

[Your name]
```

---

### Leadership Change (New VP Revenue / CRO)

**Subject:** `Welcome to [Company name] — one question`

```
Hi [First name],

Congrats on the new role at [Company name]. First 90 days in a [CRO / VP Revenue] seat
are usually about understanding what's working in pipeline and what isn't.

We help revenue leaders get that clarity fast — without months of data cleanup.

Would love to share what we've seen work in similar transitions. Worth a conversation?

[Your name]
```

---

### Product Launch

**Subject:** `[Product name] launch → pipeline question`

```
Hi [First name],

Saw the [Product name] launch — nice work. New products usually mean new GTM motions,
which means figuring out what's actually driving pipeline for the new offering vs. legacy.

That's exactly the problem we help solve. Worth 20 minutes to see if there's a fit?

[Your name]
```

## 5. A/B Test Framework

Run structured A/B tests on sequences. Track results before changing copy.

| Variable | Option A | Option B | Metric | Minimum Sample |
|----------|---------|---------|--------|---------------|
| Subject line | Question format | Statement format | Open rate | 50 sends each |
| First line | Trigger event | Role-specific pain | Reply rate | 50 sends each |
| CTA | "Worth a call?" | "20 minutes this week?" | Meeting book rate | 50 sends each |
| Email length | <80 words | 100–120 words | Reply rate | 50 sends each |
| Send time | Tuesday 8–9 AM | Thursday 7–8 AM | Open rate | 100 sends each |

**Rules:**
- Change ONE variable at a time
- Run for minimum 2 weeks before declaring a winner
- Document results in the learning loop (prospecting-plan.md Section 6)
- Winning variant becomes the default; archive losing variant with notes

## 6. Response Handling

### Positive Reply — Interested

**Immediate action (within 2 hours):**
1. Reply to confirm availability
2. Send calendar link or propose 2–3 specific times
3. Log in CRM: status → Meeting Booked
4. Prepare discovery call: run pre-call checklist from discovery-prep.md
5. Send confirmation email with agenda 24 h before call

**Reply template:**
```
Great to hear from you, [First name].

[Confirm meeting time or offer times.] I'll send a calendar invite.

To make the most of our time, I'll come prepared with [brief context on what you'll cover].
Looking forward to it.

[Your name]
```

---

### Objection — "We already have a solution"

```
Totally fair — what are you using? [Pause for reply]

Most teams we talk to have something in place but hit a specific gap around [X].
If that's not the case for you, no need to take up your time.

If it is — worth 20 minutes to compare notes?
```

---

### Objection — "Not the right time / budget"

```
Understood — timing matters.

When does [company name]'s budget cycle typically open up?
I'll follow up then rather than bother you now.

[Optionally: drop a single useful resource and sign off warmly.]
```

---

### Referral Request

```
No problem at all — appreciate you being straight with me.

Is there anyone on your team or in your network this might be more relevant for?
Happy to reach out to them directly so I'm not clogging your inbox.
```

---

### Not Now — Future Opportunity

```
Totally get it. I'll make a note to circle back in [N months] when timing might work better.

In the meantime, [optional: share one resource or insight].

Best,
[Your name]
```

**CRM action:** Set task for follow-up in [N months]; tag "Nurture"; note specific reason and timing preference.
