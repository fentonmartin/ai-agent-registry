# Qualification Frameworks Reference

Source: zarif3624/gtm-skills qualify-opportunity

## Framework-Neutral Dimensions (Default)

| Dimension | What to establish | Score options |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Specific current-state pain exists | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Impact | Consequence and priority understood | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Outcome | Measurable success defined | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| People | Users, champion, approvers, economic buyer, blockers mapped | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Decision | Criteria, process, and required validation known | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Commercial | Budget, procurement, legal, security, timing understood | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Competition | Alternatives and cost of inaction understood | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |
| Mutual action | Both sides have accepted next steps | Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Contradicted |

## MEDDPICC

| Letter | Stands for | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| M | Metrics | What measurable value does the customer expect? |
| E | Economic Buyer | Who controls the budget and final decision? |
| D | Decision Criteria | What are the formal and informal criteria? |
| D | Decision Process | What steps and approvals are required? |
| P | Paper Process | What does the contract/legal/procurement process look like? |
| I | Identify Pain | What is the specific, quantified business problem? |
| C | Champion | Who actively sells internally on our behalf? |
| C | Competition | What alternatives are they considering? |

## SPICED

| Letter | Stands for | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| S | Situation | What is their current state? |
| P | Pain | What problem do they need to solve? |
| I | Impact | What is the business impact of the pain? |
| C | Critical Event | What deadline or event creates urgency? |
| D | Decision | Who decides, how, and when? |

## BANT

| Letter | Stands for | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| B | Budget | Do they have allocated budget? |
| A | Authority | Can this person sign? |
| N | Need | Is there a real, qualified need? |
| T | Timeline | When do they need to decide? |

## Recommendations

| Signal | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 6+ dimensions Confirmed | **Advance** to next stage |
| 4–5 Confirmed, 2–3 Partial | **Continue discovery** — fill gaps |
| 3+ Unknown on high-impact dimensions | **Hold** — need more evidence |
| Problem or Champion = Contradicted | **Disqualify** |
| Champion = Unknown after 2 meetings | **Hold** or **Disqualify** |

## Evidence Rules
- **Buyer statement** (said in call/email) > **Seller assumption** (inferred)
- **CRM default** is NOT confirmed qualification — it is a field with a default value
- A fast No is better than a slow No — disqualification frees resources
