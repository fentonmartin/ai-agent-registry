# Market Sizing Reference

Source: zarif3624/gtm-skills, louisblythe/Sales-Skills, berani-ai-skills

## TAM / SAM / SOM Framework

### Definitions
- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market): Total revenue opportunity if 100% market share
- **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): Segment TAM can realistically serve given geography, motion, and channel
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic capture in 12–36 months given competition, capacity, and GTM

### Methods

#### Bottom-Up (Preferred)
```
TAM = # companies matching ICP profile × avg deal size × buying frequency
SAM = TAM × addressable geography × accessible channel
SOM = SAM × realistic win rate × capacity constraint
```

#### Top-Down (Sanity Check)
```
TAM = industry report total × relevance filter
SAM = TAM × serviceable % (your motion can reach)
SOM = SAM × market share target (12–36 months)
```

### Evidence Labels
- **Fact**: cited from Statista, industry report, CRM data with source URL
- **Inference**: calculated from verified inputs, method stated
- **Assumption**: no external source — label explicitly, flag for validation

## ICP Tiering

### Tier 1 — Ideal (≥4 criteria match)
- Industry vertical match
- Company size in range
- Tech stack signal present
- Buying trigger active (funding, hiring spike, leadership change)
- Geography match

### Tier 2 — Good (2–3 criteria)
- Partial firmographic match
- No active trigger but fits profile

### Tier 3 — Possible (opportunistic)
- One criterion match
- Unknown fit — needs research before outreach

## Firmographic Filters

| Dimension | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Industry | Primary vertical + adjacencies |
| Size | Employee range + revenue range |
| Geography | Country / region / city tier |
| Stage | Startup / Growth / Enterprise |
| Tech stack | Key tools that signal fit or conflict |
| Funding | Stage + recency (signal of budget) |
| Growth | Hiring velocity as proxy |

## Buying Triggers (Prioritized)
1. New funding round (budget unlocked)
2. Leadership hire (new VP/CRO/CTO — 90-day window)
3. Hiring spike in relevant function
4. Product launch / expansion announcement
5. Technology migration signal
6. Regulatory change in their industry
7. Competitor win in their account base
