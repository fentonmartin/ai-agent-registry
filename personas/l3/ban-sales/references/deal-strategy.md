## Deal Risk Signals

| Signal | Risk level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| No activity 14+ days | High | Re-engage or disqualify |
| Single-threaded (1 contact) | High | Multi-thread immediately |
| Champion not confirmed | High | Find or build champion |
| Close date in the past | High | Reset or remove from forecast |
| No budget confirmed | Medium | Qualify commercial path |
| Competitor not mapped | Medium | Research alternatives |
| Mutual action plan missing | Medium | Create MAP in next meeting |

## Forecast Categories

| Category | Definition | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed Won** | Signed — revenue recognized | 100% |
| **Commit** | Champion + EB confirmed; MAP agreed; no blocker | 80–90% |
| **Best Case** | Strong fit, progressing, but 1–2 gaps | 50–70% |
| **Pipeline** | Qualified, early stage | 20–40% |
| **Omit** | Stale, unqualified, or at risk | <20% |

## Win/Loss Categories

| Category | Signals | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Product gap | "Can't do X" repeatedly | Product roadmap |
| Competitive loss | Chose competitor | Competitive intel → Ban-Sales-Pitch battle card |
| Pricing | "Too expensive" without ROI conversation | ROI calculator → Ban-Sales-Pitch |
| Champion failure | Internal champion lost authority or left | Multi-thread earlier |
| Timing | "Not now" — no critical event | ICP trigger refinement → Ban-Sales-Market |
| Process failure | Deal stalled, no MAP | MAP discipline |
