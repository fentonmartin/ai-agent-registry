# Discovery Prep — Call Preparation & Execution Framework

> Use this before every discovery call. A poorly prepared discovery wastes the buyer's time
> and yours. Preparation is the highest-leverage 30 minutes before any meeting.

---

## 1. Pre-Call Checklist

Complete every item at least 24 hours before the call.

### Account Research
- [ ] Account brief read in full (account-brief-template.md)
- [ ] Company news from last 30 days reviewed (funding, hires, product launches)
- [ ] LinkedIn profiles reviewed for each known attendee
- [ ] Trigger event that prompted outreach — confirmed and documented
- [ ] Known tech stack / tools documented (from public sources, job postings)
- [ ] ICP tier confirmed; any new signals since initial research?
- [ ] Competitors they may be using — identified or hypothesized

### Logistics
- [ ] Meeting invite accepted by all attendees
- [ ] Attendee names and titles confirmed (ask in confirmation email if unclear)
- [ ] Video link tested
- [ ] Call objective written down (see Section 2)
- [ ] Primary question list prepared (max 8–10 questions)
- [ ] Account brief shared with AE or other internal attendees
- [ ] CRM record updated with known info before the call

### Materials (if applicable)
- [ ] Relevant case study or proof point identified (matching their industry/stage)
- [ ] Any demo or visual prepared — only if requested in advance
- [ ] Pricing floor / ceiling approved by AE (for Stage 2+ calls only)

## 2. Call Objective Types

Define ONE primary objective before every call. Write it in the notes template.

| Objective Type | When to Use | Success Looks Like |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| **Diagnose pain** | First discovery call; unknown depth of problem | Buyer articulates the problem in their own words; you can score it against qualification framework |
| **Validate ICP fit** | Tier 2 account or incomplete ICP signals | Confirmed or rejected 2+ Must-have criteria from icp-framework.md |
| **Advance stage** | Already diagnosed; need next step commitment | Buyer agrees to next specific action (demo, technical review, stakeholder intro) |
| **Multi-stakeholder mapping** | Single contact engaged; need to expand | Buyer names and describes other stakeholders; intro arranged |
| **Competitive displacement** | Known incumbent; need to understand switching triggers | Understand incumbent's weaknesses and buyer's switching threshold |

**Rule:** If you can't state your objective in one sentence before the call, reschedule and prepare more.

## 3. Agenda Templates

Always share agenda in the confirmation email 24 hours before. Set expectations; respect their time.

### 30-Minute Discovery Agenda

```
[Meeting name] — [Date] [Time]

Agenda (30 min):
1. Introductions — 3 min
2. Their context: what's going on at [Company name] — 10 min
3. Our context: what we do and who we typically help — 5 min
4. Fit assessment: questions from both sides — 8 min
5. Next steps — 4 min

Goal: Determine if there's a fit worth exploring further.
```

### 45-Minute Discovery Agenda

```
[Meeting name] — [Date] [Time]

Agenda (45 min):
1. Introductions — 3 min
2. Their situation and priorities — 12 min
3. Deep-dive on [specific pain area confirmed in outreach] — 12 min
4. Our approach and relevant proof points — 8 min
5. Fit discussion: questions from both sides — 6 min
6. Next steps — 4 min
```

### 60-Minute Discovery + Initial Scoping Agenda

```
[Meeting name] — [Date] [Time]

Agenda (60 min):
1. Introductions and goals for the call — 5 min
2. Company context and current state — 10 min
3. Problem deep-dive (pain, impact, urgency) — 15 min
4. Current approach and what's not working — 10 min
5. Our solution overview (relevant only) — 10 min
6. Preliminary fit assessment — 5 min
7. Next steps and process — 5 min
```

## 4. Question Bank — SPIN Framework

Ask questions in this order. Don't interrogate — let it flow as a conversation.
**Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-payoff**

### Situation Questions (understand current state)

- "Walk me through how [the specific process] works today at [Company name]."
- "Who owns [the problem area] on your team right now?"
- "What tools are you using for [relevant function]? How long have you been on those?"
- "How large is the [RevOps / Growth / Sales] team today, and how has it grown in the past year?"
- "What does your current reporting process look like for [specific metric]?"
- "How are you currently tracking [pipeline / attribution / activation]?"

### Problem Questions (surface friction and gaps)

- "What's the hardest part of [current process] for your team right now?"
- "Where does [the process] tend to break down or slow you down?"
- "If I asked your [analyst / SDR / RevOps analyst] what wastes the most time, what would they say?"
- "What's the one thing about [current approach] you'd change first if you could?"
- "Are there any decisions you can't make right now because you don't have the right data?"
- "How confident are you in the [pipeline numbers / attribution data] you report to leadership?"

### Implication Questions (deepen impact and urgency)

- "What happens to [the business goal] if that problem stays unsolved for another quarter?"
- "How does [the gap] affect [downstream team or metric]?"
- "When [the problem occurs], how much time does it take to fix it manually?"
- "What does that mean in terms of headcount or cost? Have you tried to estimate it?"
- "If you don't solve this, what's the fallback plan? Is there one?"
- "How does this affect your ability to [hit revenue targets / scale the team / report to the board]?"

### Need-Payoff Questions (surface value and buyer motivation)

- "If [the problem] were solved, what would that change for your team day to day?"
- "What would it mean for you personally if this was sorted out in the next quarter?"
- "If you could show leadership [specific outcome], how would that change your priorities?"
- "In an ideal world, what does a solved version of this look like?"
- "How would you measure success 6 months after implementing a solution?"
- "What would a strong business case for solving this look like for your CFO?"

## 5. Active Listening Signals

Watch for and note these during the call. They signal real pain, urgency, or risk.

| Signal | What It Means | Action |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Buyer uses specific numbers ("we spend 12 hours a week on this") | High-confidence pain statement | Note verbatim; use in qualification and follow-up |
| Unprompted escalation ("my CEO asked about this last week") | Executive-level urgency | Probe: "What specifically did they ask?" |
| Emotional language ("it's honestly embarrassing") | Deep pain; strong motivation | Note tone; this is a strong buying signal |
| Hedging ("we've tried to fix this before…") | Previous failure; skepticism | Probe: "What happened? What made it fail?" |
| Name-dropping ("like what [competitor] does") | They've done research; know the space | Probe: "Have you talked to them? What did you think?" |
| Silence after a question | Processing; may indicate discomfort | Wait. Don't fill silence with more talking. |
| Redirecting questions ("what about your pricing?") | Ready to advance; premature close signal | Note and return to after completing diagnosis |

## 7. Post-Call Update Protocol

Complete these updates within 24 hours of call.

### CRM Updates
- [ ] Contact record updated: attendees, titles, email, phone confirmed
- [ ] Deal stage updated based on call outcome
- [ ] Next action logged as a task with due date and owner
- [ ] Call notes pasted into deal record (summary version; keep verbatim in this doc)
- [ ] Qualification status updated in deal record

### GTM Context Update (gtm-context-template.md)
- [ ] Add any new buyer language to "Buyer Language" section
- [ ] Update "Problems Confirmed" if new pain articulation emerged
- [ ] Note any new competitive intel
- [ ] Flag any ICP signals that differ from current ICP definition

### Qualification Matrix Update (qualification-frameworks.md)
- [ ] Score updated criteria from this call
- [ ] Advance stage if 3+ criteria now confirmed
- [ ] Flag for ICP review if account fails 2+ Must-have criteria (consider disqualifying)

### Team Communication
- [ ] Slack/email AE with call summary if not present on call
- [ ] Flag any at-risk or unusual signals to manager within 24 h
- [ ] Share buyer statements with marketing if they contain new positioning insight
